# Contributing Corrections and Results

This repository prioritizes exact statements, traceable proofs, and reproducibility.

## Mathematical contributions

For every proposed result, include:
- a complete statement with all assumptions and quantifiers;
- a proof with cited dependencies;
- a clear label: proved, conditional, computationally checked, candidate, or open;
- examples or counterexamples where useful;
- source/version information if the result comes from an archived manuscript.

## Computational contributions

Include runnable code, software versions, parameters, expected outputs, actual outputs, precision, and known limitations. Identify the exact finite range checked. Do not present finite verification as a proof for an unbounded family without a separate argument.

## Editing policy

Prefer focused changes that preserve the historical statement and explain what changed. Do not silently replace theorem hypotheses or upgrade a proof status. If a proof gap is found, document it plainly and link the affected result in the theorem index.

## Research scope

Do not claim that a result proves a broader conjecture unless the complete implication chain is included and checked.
